# Generated audio fixtures

These seven short WAV files were synthesized specifically for this project. They contain mathematical sine tones with short amplitude envelopes, not sampled music or instrument recordings. All example audio and `scripts/generate_examples.py` fixture output are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

Regenerate with `python scripts/generate_examples.py` after installing backend requirements. Cases: sustained A4, C-major melody, two-note chord, low guitar scale, bass riff, piano chords, and a mixture of low/high simultaneous pitches. `mixed_pitches.wav` tests overlapping pitches; it is not a realistic instrument-separation benchmark.
